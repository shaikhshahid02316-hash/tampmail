What Is a Temporary Email?

A temporary email is an automatically generated email address that exists for a limited time. It is mainly used for receiving verification emails, OTP codes, confirmation links, and test messages without using a real email account.

Unlike permanent email services, a temporary email does not require registration, passwords, or personal information.

Why Do People Use Temporary Email?

  • To receive OTPs without sharing personal email
  • To avoid spam and promotional emails
  • For account verification on websites and apps
  • For job applications and online shopping
  • For software testing and development
