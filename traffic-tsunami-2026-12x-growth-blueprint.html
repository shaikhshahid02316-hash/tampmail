Framework 4: The Schema Stack

Google trusts pages that speak its language. Every page needs at least 3 schema types:

  • Article Schema: For blog posts and guides
  • FAQ Schema: For Q&A sections (featured snippets)
  • Breadcrumb Schema: For navigation and site structure
  • Review Schema: For social proof and star ratings
  • Organization Schema: For brand authority
