What is Temporary Email?

Temporary email is a short-term email address that can be used to receive emails without using your personal or real email address. It is also called disposable email or temp mail.

Why Temporary Email Is Used

People use temporary email to avoid spam, protect privacy, and stay safe online. Many websites ask for email verification, and using a temporary email helps keep your main inbox clean.

How Temporary Email Works

A temporary email service automatically creates an email address for you. Any email sent to this address appears instantly in the inbox without signup or password.

Is Temporary Email Safe?

Temporary email is safe for non-personal use such as website registration, app testing, and receiving verification codes. It should not be used for banking or important accounts.

When Should You Use Disposable Email?

Disposable email should be used when signing up for free trials, downloading files, joining forums, or testing online services where privacy is important.
