Understanding Temporary Emails

Temporary emails are email addresses designed to be used for a limited period. They are often deployed for situations where users need to access content or services without revealing their permanent email address. By acting as a shield, temporary emails help protect against spam, phishing attempts, and identity theft.

Key Features of Temporary Emails

  • Short lifespan, usually ranging from 10 minutes to a few days.
  • No need for registration or personal information.
  • Automatic deletion after expiration, ensuring data minimization.
