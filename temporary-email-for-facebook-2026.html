How Temporary Email Works Technically

When you visit TampMail.xyz, our system generates a random email address instantly. Here's what happens behind the scenes:

  1. Random Generation: A unique email address is created using cryptographic random generators (example: user-fb7k2@tampmail.xyz)
  2. SMTP Reception: When Facebook sends an email to this address, our mail servers receive it
  3. RAM Storage: The email is stored temporarily in volatile memory – never written to disk
  4. Inbox Display: You can view the email through a unique, unguessable URL
  5. Auto-Deletion: After 24 hours, all data is cryptographically erased – no backups, no recovery
🔧 Technical Note: This RAM-only architecture means even if our servers were seized, no historical email data exists. This is privacy by design.
