Frequently Asked Questions

What is a disposable email?

A disposable email is a temporary email address that expires after a specific period or after a user-defined purpose is fulfilled.

Are disposable emails secure?

They offer enhanced privacy but are not designed for sensitive data. Use them for low-risk activities like signing up for services.

Can disposable emails replace traditional email accounts?

No, disposable emails are designed to complement traditional email accounts, not replace them. They are ideal for temporary or anonymous communication.
