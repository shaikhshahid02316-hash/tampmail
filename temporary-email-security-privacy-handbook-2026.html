1. The real threats to your email privacy (that you probably ignore)

  • Data breaches: Every time you share your real email, you expose it to potential hacks. Once leaked, your email lands on spam lists and dark web marketplaces.
  • AI‑powered tracking: Companies now use machine learning to link your email across services, building a complete profile of your behaviour, purchases, and interests.
  • Spam avalanches: One newsletter signup can lead to hundreds of promotional emails – and unsubscribing often confirms your address is active, making it worse.
  • Phishing attacks: Attackers target known email addresses with convincing scams. The more places your email appears, the higher the risk.
  • Account takeover: With your email and a leaked password from another service, hackers can attempt to access your bank, social media, or work accounts.

2. How temporary email acts as a shield (the 5‑layer defence)

  1. Identity separation: Each temporary email is a unique, unconnected identity. A leak at one service doesn’t affect others.
  2. Automatic expiry: After 24 hours, the address and all messages are cryptographically erased – no data lingers.
  3. Zero‑log architecture: TampMail doesn’t store IP addresses, timestamps, or metadata. There’s nothing to trace back to you.
  4. Domain rotation: Our system automatically switches domains, preventing platform‑side blocklists from flagging you.
  5. No personal data collection: You never give your name, phone, or any identifier – complete anonymity by design.
🔐 The result: Even if a service you signed up for gets hacked, the attacker only sees a dead, anonymous email address that self‑destructs. Your real inbox remains untouched.
