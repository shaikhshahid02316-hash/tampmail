📧 What is Temp Mail? (Complete Definition)

Temp mail (temporary email, disposable email, or burner email) is a short-lived email address designed for one-time or limited use. Unlike Gmail or Outlook, it requires no registration, no personal information, and no password. You get an instant inbox that receives emails and then self-destructs – typically after 24 hours.

Key Characteristics

  • ✅ Instant Access: No signup – just visit and copy
  • ✅ Complete Anonymity: No phone, name, or ID required
  • ✅ Auto-Deletion: Messages vanish after 24 hours
  • ✅ Zero Logs: Your IP and metadata are never stored
  • ✅ Receive Only: Designed for getting OTPs, confirmations, not sending

⚙️ How Temp Mail Works Technically

Behind the simple interface lies sophisticated infrastructure:

  1. Random Generation: Algorithms create unpredictable addresses (e.g., `x7f3k9@tampmail.xyz`).
  2. SMTP Reception: Our servers accept emails via standard SMTP protocol.
  3. RAM-Only Storage: Emails live in volatile memory – never on disk.
  4. Unique URL: You access your inbox via a private, unguessable link.
  5. 24-Hour TTL: After 24 hours, data is cryptographically erased.
