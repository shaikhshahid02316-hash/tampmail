1. The New Threats You Can't Ignore (2026 Edition)

Five years ago, spam was the main annoyance. Today, the stakes are much higher:

  • AI‑powered cross‑platform tracking: Companies like Meta, Google, and Amazon use machine learning to link your email across different services, building a detailed profile of your behaviour, purchases, and interests.
  • Data broker databases: Your email is bought and sold by companies like Acxiom, Oracle, and LiveRamp. Once they have it, they can correlate your online activity across thousands of sites – even in incognito mode.
  • Credential stuffing attacks: Hackers take leaked email/password combinations from one breach and try them on banking, social media, and e‑commerce sites. Using your real email everywhere multiplies your risk.
  • Endless spam and phishing: The average person receives 45 spam emails per day. Each one is a potential phishing attempt designed to steal your login details.
💡 The solution: Use a unique, disposable email address for every non‑critical signup. This breaks the chain – a leak at one service won't affect others, and data brokers see only fragmented, useless data.
