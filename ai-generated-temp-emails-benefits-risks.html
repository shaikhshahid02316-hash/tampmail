Chapter 2: Risks and Challenges

While AI-generated temporary emails offer numerous benefits, they are not without risks. Users must be aware of potential challenges to effectively mitigate them.

Potential Risks

  • Data breaches due to malicious actors targeting AI systems.
  • Over-reliance on AI could lead to reduced user control.
  • False positives in spam detection could block legitimate emails.
  • Privacy concerns stemming from AI profiling.

Mitigation Strategies

  1. Regular audits of AI systems to ensure security compliance.
  2. Implementation of robust encryption standards.
  3. User education on safe practices for temporary email usage.
  4. Collaboration with cybersecurity experts for proactive threat detection.
